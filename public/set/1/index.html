Set intel

Confirmed by Jonin from collectors and trade sources. Know something we don’t?

Rarity & print run

  • Launch sell-out

    The Path to Hokage hit hobby stores April 28, 2006 and the first booster run sold out at most distributors, which got roughly half of what they ordered. Blister boosters were diverted to hobby stores, and mass retail (Toys R Us, Walmart, Target) got product in June. Blister and hobby packs have the same cards and insert rates.

    Source: ICv2
  • Unlimited reprint

    A second, Unlimited printing of Series 1 boosters, without the First Edition mark and with minor text corrections, was due at retail in September 2006.

    Source: ICv2

1st Edition

  • Starter deck cards are unmarked

    Series 1 booster cards carry the First Edition mark, but the starter-deck cards do not. An unmarked Set 1 card can be a starter-deck copy, not only Unlimited.

    Source: ICv2 (Bandai interview)
