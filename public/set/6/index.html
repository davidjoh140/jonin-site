Rarity & print run

  • 80 US-only cards

    Eternal Rivalry has 113 cards, 80 of them with art and effects never released in Japan, plus some Japanese vending-machine limited cards. Hobby street date July 27, 2007. Packs held 1 holo foil, 1 gold-stamped Rare, 2 Uncommons and 6 Commons.

    Source: ICv2
