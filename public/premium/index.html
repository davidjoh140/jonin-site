Questions

Is there a chat?

No. The Discord is alerts only, so it stays quiet and easy to scan.

How fast are the alerts?

Most come through within minutes of the post or listing going up.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

Do you earn from the eBay links?

Some eBay links are affiliate links, so Jonin may earn a small commission at no cost to you.
