Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Jonin is a fan-made database for the original Bandai Naruto CCG. We keep the information we collect to a minimum and never sell it.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you make an account: your email address, the username you pick, and your password (stored securely by our sign-in provider; we never see it). If you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from that service, and we create a username from your name.
  • What you add to the site. Your collection (haves, wants, slabs), card votes, and any settings you choose, such as whether your collection list is shared publicly.
  • Feedback. Anything you send through the Feedback button, including a name or contact if you choose to give one.
  • Anonymous usage. Page views, searches and cards opened, tied to a random ID stored in your browser, not to your name or account. We use this to see which pages and cards people use.
  • Your browser's storage. We keep a few things in your browser so the site remembers them, such as your theme, a collection you started before signing up, and whether you're signed in.

How we use it

To run your account, save your collection and votes, show vote totals and rankings (as totals only, never who voted for what), send you alerts you ask for, answer feedback, and improve the site. We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and site data.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site.
  • Google and Discord, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • eBay. Some links to eBay are affiliate links. If you click one, eBay may use cookies to credit Jonin with a small commission. We don't receive your eBay details.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your collection and votes at any time on the site. To delete your account and everything tied to it, email david@jonin.cards from the address on your account and we'll remove it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: david@jonin.cards.
