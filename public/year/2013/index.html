Questions

What is my 2013 Naruto card worth?

It depends which card you have. Bandai printed 2 Naruto Uzumaki cards in 2013, and the ones with recorded sales go for $7,000–$14,000, depending on the card, edition, foil and grade. The highest seller so far is Naruto Uzumaki (9 Tails Cloak) N-1646 ($7,000–$14,000). Find your exact card in the list above to see its price.

What is my 2013 Itachi card worth?

It depends which card you have. Bandai printed 1 Itachi Uchiha card in 2013, and the ones with recorded sales go for about $270, depending on the card, edition, foil and grade. The highest seller so far is Itachi Uchiha N-1677 (about $270). Find your exact card in the list above to see its price.

What is my 2013 Sakura card worth?

It depends which card you have. Bandai printed 1 Sakura Haruno card in 2013, and the ones with recorded sales go for $1,400–$1,700, depending on the card, edition, foil and grade. The highest seller so far is Sakura Haruno N-1655 ($1,400–$1,700). Find your exact card in the list above to see its price.

Are 2013 Naruto cards worth anything?

Most commons and uncommons sell for a few dollars. Super Rares, promos and graded copies of popular characters are where the value is. See what your Naruto cards are worth for how to check yours.
