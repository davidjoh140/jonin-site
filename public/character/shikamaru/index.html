Shikamaru Nara cards

Shikamaru Nara appears on 47 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 29 sets from The Path to Hokage (2006) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 10 Super Rares, 1 Promo, 11 Rares, 8 Uncommon, 1 Starter, 16 Common.

The lazy genius of the Nara clan, who finds everything a drag but out-thinks nearly every opponent with his Shadow Possession Jutsu. He's a fan favorite from the original Leaf genin and appears across many sets.
