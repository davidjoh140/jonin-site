Naruto Uzumaki cards

Naruto Uzumaki appears on 144 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 36 sets from The Path to Hokage (2006) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 1 Alt Art /20, 28 Super Rares, 27 Promos, 18 Rares, 25 Uncommon, 1 Starter, 40 Common.

The loud, stubborn knucklehead who wants to become Hokage and win over a village that once shunned him. As the hero of the series and host of the Nine-Tailed Fox, Naruto has more cards than any other character, and he appears in every main set of the game.
