Kisame Hoshigaki cards

Kisame Hoshigaki appears on 23 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 18 sets from Dream Legacy (2007) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 7 Super Rares, 11 Rares, 3 Uncommon, 1 Common.

Itachi's Akatsuki partner, a shark-like swordsman of the Mist who wields the chakra-eating blade Samehada. He's a must for Akatsuki collectors.
