Deidara cards

Deidara appears on 22 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 16 sets from A New Chronicle (2009) to Hero's Ascension (2012): 6 Super Rares, 1 Promo, 7 Rares, 8 Uncommon.

An explosive-clay artist and member of the Akatsuki who believes true art is a single bang. Deidara's battles with Gaara and Sasuke made him a favorite among the Akatsuki.
