Sasuke Uchiha cards

Sasuke Uchiha appears on 102 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 36 sets from The Path to Hokage (2006) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 1 Alt Art /20, 15 Super Rares, 20 Promos, 18 Rares, 18 Uncommon, 28 Common.

The last of the Uchiha clan, Sasuke is Naruto's rival and best friend, driven by a quest for power and revenge. His Sharingan and Chidori make him a fan favorite, and his cards run from the very first set to the last.
