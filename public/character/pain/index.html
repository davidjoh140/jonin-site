Pain cards

Pain appears on 23 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 9 sets from Path of Pain (2010) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 1 Alt Art /20, 6 Super Rares, 1 Promo, 1 Rare, 7 Uncommon, 1 Starter, 6 Common.

The leader of the Akatsuki, Pain controls six bodies through the Rinnegan, with Nagato pulling the strings behind them. His assault on the Leaf Village is one of the most famous arcs in the series, and his cards come from the later sets.
