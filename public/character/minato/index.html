Minato Namikaze cards

Minato Namikaze appears on 18 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 15 sets from Curse of the Sand (2006) to Hero's Ascension (2012): 1 Alt Art /20, 10 Super Rares, 2 Promos, 4 Rares, 1 Common.

The Fourth Hokage, known as the Yellow Flash of the Leaf, and Naruto's father. Minato appears in the game as both Minato Namikaze and The Fourth Hokage, and Jonin counts both names toward him.
