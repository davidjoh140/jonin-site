Kiba Inuzuka cards

Kiba Inuzuka appears on 32 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 23 sets from The Path to Hokage (2006) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 7 Rares, 10 Uncommon, 15 Common.

A member of Team 8 who fights side by side with his ninja dog, Akamaru. Kiba rounds out Team 8 with Hinata and Shino.
