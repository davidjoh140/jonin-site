Choji Akimichi cards

Choji Akimichi appears on 34 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 27 sets from The Path to Hokage (2006) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 4 Rares, 3 Uncommon, 1 Starter, 25 Common.

The kind-hearted Team 10 member whose Akimichi clan jutsu let him grow to giant size. Choji's loyalty to Shikamaru and his food pills are classic moments from the early series.
