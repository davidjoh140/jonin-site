Hinata Hyuga cards

Hinata Hyuga appears on 40 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 28 sets from The Path to Hokage (2006) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 1 Alt Art /20, 1 Super Rare, 1 Promo, 4 Rares, 13 Uncommon, 1 Starter, 19 Common.

The shy heiress of the Hyuga clan, Hinata quietly cheers Naruto on and fights with the Byakugan and Gentle Fist. She's one of the most loved characters in the series, and her cards are popular with collectors.
