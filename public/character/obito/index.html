Obito Uchiha cards

Obito Uchiha appears on 21 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 14 sets from Emerging Alliance (2009) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 3 Super Rares, 3 Rares, 6 Uncommon, 8 Common.

Kakashi's former teammate, long thought dead, whose story is one of the biggest twists in the series. His cards include his masked identity, Tobi, and Jonin counts both names toward Obito.
