Asuma Sarutobi cards

Asuma Sarutobi appears on 22 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 19 sets from The Path to Hokage (2006) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 1 Alt Art /20, 4 Super Rares, 5 Rares, 6 Uncommon, 6 Common.

The Team 10 sensei and son of the Third Hokage, who fights with chakra blades. Asuma's bond with Shikamaru is at the heart of one of the series' most emotional arcs.
