Orochimaru cards

Orochimaru appears on 28 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 21 sets from Coils of the Snake (2006) to Avenger's Wrath (2012): 10 Super Rares, 10 Rares, 7 Uncommon, 1 Common.

One of the Legendary Sannin, Orochimaru turned his back on the Leaf in his search for forbidden jutsu and immortality. He's the main villain of the early series and one of its most memorable.
