Kakashi Hatake cards

Kakashi Hatake appears on 76 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 36 sets from The Path to Hokage (2006) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 3 Alt Art /20, 14 Super Rares, 11 Promos, 21 Rares, 20 Uncommon, 7 Common.

Team 7's laid-back sensei, the Copy Ninja famous for his Sharingan eye, his mask and his ever-present book. Kakashi is one of the most popular characters in the series and appears from the first set to the last.
