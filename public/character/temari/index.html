Temari cards

Temari appears on 32 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 27 sets from Coils of the Snake (2006) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 3 Super Rares, 9 Rares, 7 Uncommon, 1 Starter, 12 Common.

Gaara's older sister, a wind-style fighter from the Sand Village who wields a giant fan. Temari appears from the Chunin Exams onward, often alongside her brothers Gaara and Kankuro.
