Haku cards

Haku appears on 21 cards in the original Bandai Naruto CCG, across 16 sets from The Path to Hokage (2006) to Ultimate Ninja Storm 3 (2013): 2 Super Rares, 1 Promo, 6 Rares, 5 Uncommon, 1 Starter, 6 Common.

Zabuza's loyal companion, with a rare ice style and a mirror technique that nearly defeats Team 7 in their first real mission. Haku's story is one of the most memorable of the Land of Waves arc, and he appears from the very first set.
