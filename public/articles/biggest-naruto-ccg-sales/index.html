The 5 biggest Naruto CCG sales we've tracked

Nov 25, 2026 · 1 min read

Every sale on Jonin is checked by hand before it counts. These are the five highest so far.

1. Sasuke Uchiha PR088

A Shonen Jump promo from 2013, the last year of the game. In a PSA 10, it's the top sale on the site.

2. Sasuke Uchiha N-496

Only an Uncommon, but a 1st Edition full foil in a PSA 10. Grade and foil matter more than rarity here.

3. Itachi Uchiha N-203

The highest raw sale on the list: a 1st Edition Super Rare foil from Dream Legacy.

4. Itachi Uchiha N-1511

A 1st Edition full foil from Avenger's Wrath, one of the last sets.

5. Naruto Uzumaki PR053

The top Naruto sale so far, raw.
