The 10 biggest Naruto CCG sales we've tracked

Nov 26, 2026 · 3 min read

The original Bandai Naruto CCG (2006 to 2013) is having its biggest run since the game ended. Jonin has checked 335 sales by hand so far. Seventeen of them went for $10,000 or more, spread across eight different cards, and every one of those happened since late August 2026.

Below are the 10 biggest sales in the Jonin database, one spot per card, so a card can't take two places. Each one links to the sale itself.

1. Pain (Deva Path) & Itachi Uchiha: $30,000

The top sale we've checked is a 1st Edition Super Rare from Tales of the Gallant Sage, the game's 20th set. This card has sold three times in a PSA 10 since August, climbing from $13,500 (Unlimited) to $20,000 and then $30,000 (both 1st Edition).

2. Susano'o (Sasuke): $25,000

From Avenger's Wrath (2012). With 15 checked sales, it's the most-traded card on this list. This PSA 10 went for an accepted Best Offer of $25,000.

3. Naruto Uzumaki & Sasuke Uchiha: $22,000

The Super Rare foil from The Chosen (2008), the only set with prism and spiral foils. It's the oldest card in the top three.

4. Sasuke Uchiha PR088: $18,000

A 2013 Shonen Jump Championship promo from the game's final year. It's the top promo we've checked, and it sold at auction.

5. Sasuke Uchiha, Weapons of War: $14,500

A 1st Edition full art Super Rare from set 22, sold at auction in a PSA 10.

6. Naruto Uzumaki (9 Tails Cloak): $14,000

From Ultimate Ninja Storm 3, the last set Bandai made. It's the top Naruto card on the list.

7. Naruto Uzumaki (Sage Mode): $12,500

A 1st Edition Super Rare from Sage's Legacy (set 24), in a PSA 10. It's one of three solo Naruto cards on the list.

8. Sai, Shattered Truths: $10,000

A 1st Edition full art in a PSA 10. Sai is the only character on this list who isn't Naruto, Sasuke, Itachi or Pain.

9. Naruto Uzumaki N-001: $9,999.99

Card number one from the very first set, The Path to Hokage (2006), in diamond foil. It's the only PSA 9 on the list. Even a grade down, the first Naruto card is close to five figures.

10. Sasuke Uchiha, Shattered Truths: $9,800

Another 1st Edition full art from set 21, sold at auction.

Some of these have come down

A record is the most someone has paid, not the price you have to pay. Since setting their highs, several of these cards have sold for less in the same grade:

  • Susano'o (Sasuke), PSA 10: $14,700 on Nov 22 and $12,600 on Nov 23, against its $25,000 record. As of Nov 26, one is listed for $18,000.
  • Sasuke Uchiha PR088, PSA 10: $6,050 on Nov 17, against its $18,000 record.
  • Sasuke Uchiha, Weapons of War, 1st Edition PSA 10: $9,800 on Nov 12, against $14,500.
  • Naruto Uzumaki (9 Tails Cloak), PSA 10: $13,000 on Nov 1, against $14,000.

If you're chasing one of these, check the card's page for its recent sales, or set a Jonin Club alert to hear when a copy lists.

What the list tells us

  • Grade is everything. Nine of the 10 sales are PSA 10s.
  • Late sets lead. Five spots go to 1st Edition full arts from sets 20 to 24 (Tales of the Gallant Sage, Shattered Truths, Weapons of War and Sage's Legacy).
  • Sasuke owns the top end. Sasuke shows up four times, five if you count Susano'o.
  • Naruto is climbing. Naruto has three cards of his own on the list, from the very first card in the game to the last set.

Prices on the list are final sale prices. For Best Offer sales, we use the accepted offer, not the asking price.
