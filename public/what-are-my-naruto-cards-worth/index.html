Naruto CCG card values

What are my Naruto cards worth?

Most original Bandai Naruto CCG cards sell for a few dollars. The money is in Super Rares, promos, 1st Editions, foils and graded copies of popular characters. Here's how to tell what yours is worth.

Step 1

Read your card

Four things decide what a Naruto card is worth.

  1. Card numberLike N-934 or J-001. It tells you exactly which card you have.
  2. Rarity dotsAfter the copyright line. C none · Uncommon · Rare · Super Rare
  3. Edition and foil1st Edition or Unlimited, and normal or foil: Foil types
  4. ConditionRaw (ungraded) or graded, like a PSA 10. A high grade can be worth many times a raw copy.
