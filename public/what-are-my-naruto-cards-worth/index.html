Naruto CCG card values

What are my Naruto cards worth?

Most Naruto cards sell for a few dollars. Rare cards, foils, 1st Editions and graded cards can be worth a lot more. Here's how to check yours.

Step 1

Read your card

Here's where to look, on a real card.

  1. Card typeTop left: Ninja, Jutsu or Mission. It's the N, J or M in the card number.
  2. Card numberBottom left. This one is 085, so it's N-085.
  3. Set symbolRight after the number. Each set has its own. This gourd means Curse of the Sand.
  4. Rarity dotsAfter the copyright line.CCommonUncommonRareSuper Rare
  5. FoilDoes it shine? Foils sell for more than normal copies.FlatDiamondWavySpiralPrism
Edition1st Edition or Unlimited. Each card page shows which editions it came in.
ConditionRaw (not graded) or graded, like PSA 10. High grades sell for much more.
