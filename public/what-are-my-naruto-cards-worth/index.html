Questions

Naruto card values, answered

What are my Naruto cards worth?

Most original Naruto CCG commons and uncommons sell for a few dollars. Super Rares, promos, 1st Editions, certain foils and graded copies of popular characters are worth the most. Of the 199 cards with a recorded sale on Jonin, 80 have sold for $200+ and 49 have never sold for more than $10.

Are Kayou Naruto cards covered?

No. Jonin covers the original Bandai Naruto CCG (2006–2013). Kayou and other newer Naruto cards are different products.
