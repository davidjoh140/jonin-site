What are my Naruto cards worth?

Most original Bandai Naruto CCG cards sell for a few dollars. The money is in Super Rares, promos, 1st Editions, certain foils and graded copies of popular characters. 199 cards have a recorded sale on Jonin so far, and 80 of them have sold for $200+.

How to tell what your Naruto card is worth

1. Check it's the original Bandai game. Jonin covers the Bandai Naruto CCG, printed 2006–2013. Newer Naruto cards, like Kayou, are a different product.

2. Find the card number and set. Every card has a number like N-934 or J-001. Match it in its set, or start from the year it came out: 20062007200820092010201120122013

3. Check the rarity. Look for the dots after the copyright line: none is Common, one Uncommon, two Rare and three Super Rare. Super Rares and promos are the cards most collectors chase.

4. Check the edition and foil. The same card can be 1st Edition or Unlimited, and many came in more than one finish: flat foil, diamond, wavy, spiral or prism (foil types explained). Each version sells for a different price.

5. Check condition and grading. Graded copies, especially PSA 10s, can sell for many times the price of a raw copy. Every card page links its PSA pop report and eBay sold listings for raw and graded copies.

6. Look up the price. Each card page shows what it sells for, from real sales checked by hand. Jonin Club breaks that down by edition, foil and grade, with every sold listing.
