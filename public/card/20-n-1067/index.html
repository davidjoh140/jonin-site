About this version

A full-art Super Rare. US full-art Super Rares often have a textured surface, but not all of them do, so compare photos when buying. A limited Alt Art /20 version of this card also exists: Jiraiya (Sage Mode) N-1067-ALT. It was printed in 1st Edition and Unlimited, which sell separately. Printing details for this set are still being confirmed.
