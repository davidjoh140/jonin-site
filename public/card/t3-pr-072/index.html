When this Ninja is deployed, you can attach 1 Jutsu card from your hand to this Ninja.

Valid: During the Exchange of Jutsu, you can pay 2 Chakra. In that case, add a copy of the attached Jutsu to the chain with this Ninja as the user (Requirements/target must be met/selected).
