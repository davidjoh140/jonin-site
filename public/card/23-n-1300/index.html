About this version

This Uncommon comes in two finishes: a normal card and a flat foil (sometimes called full foil). From Approaching Wind on, flat foil was the only foil finish. It was printed in 1st Edition and Unlimited, which sell separately. Printing details for this set are still being confirmed.
