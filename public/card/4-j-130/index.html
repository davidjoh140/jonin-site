About this version

This Uncommon comes three ways: a normal (non-foil) card, a diamond foil and a wavy foil. On these foils the card name stays non-foil. Listings often just say "foil", so check which pattern the photos show: the two don't always sell for the same price. It was printed in 1st Edition and Unlimited, which sell separately.
