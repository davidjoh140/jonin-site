Naruto CCG price guide

What original Bandai Naruto CCG cards (2006–2013) are worth, from real sold listings. Every sale is checked by hand before it counts. 199 cards have a recorded sale so far, and the guide updates on its own as new sales come in.

What makes a Naruto CCG card valuable

Rarity. Look for the dots after the copyright line: none is Common, one Uncommon, two Rare and three Super Rare. Super Rares and promos are the cards most collectors chase.

Edition. The same card can be 1st Edition or Unlimited. Check the card page for which editions each card was printed in.

Foil. Many cards came in more than one finish: flat foil, diamond, wavy, spiral or prism. The same card can sell for very different prices depending on its foil.

Grade and condition. Graded copies, especially PSA 10s, can sell for many times the price of a raw copy.
