Questions

Is there a chat?

No. The Discord is alerts only, so it stays quiet and easy to scan.

What is the Alt Art /20 sniper?

Tales of the Gallant Sage has 20 limited alternate-art cards, with only 20 copies of each. Some sellers list one with a regular title because they don't know what it is. The sniper checks the photos of every new listing for those 20 cards and pings members the moment one matches.

How fast are the alerts?

Most come through within minutes of the post or listing going up.

How much is Jonin Club?

$9.99 a month, or $89 a year. The first 20 accounts got it free, forever, as founding members.

How do I earn a free month?

Reach the Jonin rank (300 scrolls) for one free month, and Kage (750 scrolls) for another. Scrolls come from missions: voting on every character, logging 100 cards, feedback we use and friends you invite.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

Do you earn from the eBay links?

Some eBay links are affiliate links, so Jonin may earn a small commission at no cost to you.
